# 🎰 Slot Machine Project

Welcome to the **Slot Machine Project**! This is a fun and interactive command-line game built with Python. Try your luck, place your bets, and spin to win! 🍀💰

---

## ✨ Features

- 💸 **Deposit Funds** – Start with a minimum of $500 to play!
- 🎯 **Choose Lines** – Bet on 1 to 3 lines for more chances to win.
- 🪙 **Set Bet Amount** – Decide your bet for each line.
- 🎲 **Random Slot Spins** – Get different symbols every time!
- 🏆 **Win or Lose** – See your results and updated wallet balance.
- 🖥️ **Simple Terminal UI** – Play directly from your terminal.

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Deposit Money:**  
   Enter an amount ($500 or more) to begin.

2. **Choose Lines:**  
   Decide how many lines you'll bet on (1️⃣, 2️⃣, or 3️⃣).

3. **Set Bet Amount:**  
   Place your bet per line (within your wallet balance).

4. **Spin:**  
   The slot machine spins and displays the result.

5. **Win or Lose:**  
   Winners get payouts added to their wallet. See your new balance instantly!

---

## 🎰 Symbols

The slot machine uses these symbols:
- `@` (x6) 🐵
- `#` (x8) 🍀
- `$` (x4) 💵
- `&` (x8) 🍒

---

## 🏅 Winning

You win a line when all symbols in that line are the same.
```
Payout = Number of Winning Lines × Bet Per Line
```
The more lines you bet, the higher your chance to win! 🎉
